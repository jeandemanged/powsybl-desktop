# LCC Converter Station

The details of a line commutated converter station. Open it from the info button of the [LCC Converter Stations](../tables/lcc_converter_stations.md) table, or from the other places listed in [Equipment details](index.md).

![The lcc converter station view](../../../_generated/images/detail-lcc-converter-station.png)

## Header

A link to the voltage level, and the LCC converter station symbol with the solved values and the connection check box.

## Main tab

### General

Followed by the [converter station rows](index.md#converter-station-rows), then **Power Factor** (text field).

### Connection

See [Connection](index.md#connection-section).

## Other tabs

### Extensions

The extensions that can be added: Connectable Position, Reference Priorities, Observability, Measurements, Dynamic Model.

### Properties and Aliases
