# Ground

The details of a ground. Open it from the info button of the [Grounds](../tables/grounds.md) table, or from the other places listed in [Equipment details](index.md).

![The ground view](../../../_generated/images/detail-ground.png)

## Header

A link to the voltage level, and the ground symbol with the solved values and the connection check box.
