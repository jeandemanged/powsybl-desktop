# Tie Line

The details of a tie line, made of two boundary lines. Open it from the info button of the [Tie Lines](../tables/tie_lines.md) table, or from the other places listed in [Equipment details](index.md).

![The tie line view](../../../_generated/images/detail-tie-line.png)

## Header

Links to the voltage levels of both sides, and the line symbol with the boundary point in the middle.

## Main tab

### General

| Field | Editor | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Pairing Key** | Read-only | The key that paired the two boundary lines. |
| **Boundary Line 1**, **Boundary Line 2** | Link | To the [boundary lines' details](boundary_line.md). |

### Characteristics

R, X, G1, B1, G2 and B2, read-only: they result from the boundary lines', edited there.

### Side 1, Side 2

The [Connection](index.md#connection-section) rows of each side.

## Other tabs

### Limits

The limits of the boundary lines, side by side. See [Edit operational limits](index.md#edit-operational-limits).

### Extensions

The extensions that can be added: Operating Status, Dynamic Model. CGMES Boundary Node is shown when present but can't be added.

### Properties and Aliases

See [Equipment details](index.md#edit-extensions-properties-and-aliases).
